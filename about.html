Ian A. Tausig, CSMIE
Managing Director

Ian Tausig brings over a decade of investigative expertise to complex legal, corporate, and private matters. As a licensed private investigator and board-certified Social Media Intelligence Expert, he has established Tausig & Associates as a premier investigation agency serving law firms and insurance carriers throughout California.

His law enforcement experience ensures every investigation can withstand intense legal scrutiny. As a court-qualified expert witness specializing in social media investigative techniques and digital evidence authentication, Ian regularly provides testimony in high-stakes litigation.

Since founding the agency in 2013, Ian has built a reputation for handling sensitive matters that require both technical sophistication and absolute discretion. His expertise in emerging digital investigation methods positions the firm at the forefront of modern investigative practice.
