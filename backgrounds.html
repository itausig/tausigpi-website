Background Investigations

Every major decision comes down to people. Whether you're hiring a key executive, entering a strategic partnership, or making a significant investment, success depends on the character and integrity of who you're dealing with. The problem is that standard background checks barely scratch the surface. Automated database searches miss the nuanced risks that can sink these ventures before they start.

We take a fundamentally different approach. Instead of relying on what databases happen to contain, we conduct investigative due diligence that builds a complete picture of who someone really is. We synthesize public records with human intelligence and deep digital analysis to verify credentials, examine histories, and identify the undisclosed liabilities or conflicts of interest that could become problems later.

The result is comprehensive intelligence that goes far beyond what typical vetting reveals. We uncover the relationships, financial entanglements, and past decisions that matter most when you're about to trust someone with significant responsibility or resources. This level of insight lets you make critical decisions with genuine confidence, knowing you understand both the opportunities and the risks involved.

What We Do

Our Capabilities

Executive Vetting

Deep screening for C-suite, board, and other critical appointments where character and track record matter most. We examine not just what candidates claim, but how they've actually performed under pressure, how they've treated people along the way, and whether their reputation matches their resume.

Investment Due Diligence

Focuses on the people behind potential investments because even the best business plan falls apart if the wrong people are running it. We investigate principals and key players to uncover the undisclosed risks, hidden relationships, and past decisions that could impact your investment down the line.

Reputation & Integrity Assessments

Building comprehensive profiles that reveal both professional achievements and personal history. We identify potential conflicts of interest, legal entanglements, or character issues that might not surface in standard vetting but could become significant problems later.

Verification of Credentials

Independently confirming what people claim about their education, employment history, and professional achievements. You'd be surprised how often impressive resumes contain creative interpretations of the truth.

Methodology

Our Process in Practice

Define objectives and locate all relevant sources of information to establish a comprehensive factual baseline.

Meticulously collect and document all evidence and data in a forensically sound manner to ensure its integrity.

Our team synthesizes the collected information, connecting disparate dots, corroborating facts, and extracting the strategic insights that form the core of our intelligence product.

We deliver our findings in a clear, concise, and unambiguous report, tailored to your objectives and designed to provide the actionable clarity needed for decisive action.
