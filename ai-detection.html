Separating What's Real from What Isn't

AI Detection & Content Authentication

AI-generated content is everywhere now, and it's getting harder to tell what's real. Deepfaked images and video, synthetic documents, AI-written communications, fabricated evidence. The tools to create convincing fakes are accessible to anyone, and they're showing up in litigation, insurance claims, corporate disputes, and fraud schemes. We help clients determine what's authentic and what isn't.

What We Do

Our Capabilities

AI-Generated Content Detection

Systematic analysis to identify content created or altered by artificial intelligence. We use multiple detection methodologies to determine whether text, images, or documents are genuine or machine-generated.

Deepfake Identification

Analysis of images, video, and audio to detect manipulation by AI tools. From subtle face swaps to fully synthetic media, we identify the telltale markers that separate real from fabricated.

Synthetic Document Analysis

Examining documents for signs of AI generation or manipulation. Contracts, communications, financial records. When the authenticity of a document is in question, we provide the analysis to resolve it.

Content Provenance Verification

Tracing the origin and history of digital content. Where did it come from? Has it been altered? When was it actually created? These are questions we answer with evidence, not guesswork.

Litigation Support

Expert analysis for cases involving AI-generated evidence. As courts begin grappling with synthetic content, we provide the technical expertise to help legal teams navigate this new terrain.

Insurance Fraud Detection

Identifying AI-generated documentation in insurance claims. Fabricated photos, altered medical records, synthetic supporting documents. We help carriers identify fraud before it costs them.

Ahead of the Curve

Most investigation firms are still catching up to AI-generated evidence. We've been building detection methodology since this became a practical problem in litigation — not since it became a headline. When opposing counsel submits photographic evidence, a document, or a communication that doesn't look right, we provide the analysis that resolves the question definitively. Grounded methodology, documented findings, court-ready results.
