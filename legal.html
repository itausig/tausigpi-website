Legal Investigations & Litigation Support

In high-stakes litigation, winning often comes down to who has the better facts. We work as a seamless extension of premier law firms, providing the investigative muscle needed to build cases that can't be easily torn apart, anticipate what the other side will throw at you, and negotiate from a position of real strength.

Our job starts where standard discovery leaves off. While depositions and document requests reveal what people are willing to share, we uncover what they'd rather keep hidden. We combine digital forensics, financial analysis, and old-school legwork to give legal teams the edge that changes outcomes.

The intelligence we deliver doesn't just fill gaps in your case—it's collected and documented with one eye always on the courtroom. Every piece of evidence is gathered in ways that will hold up under cross-examination and meet the standards judges expect. Because finding the smoking gun is only valuable if you can actually use it when it matters most.

What We Do

Our Capabilities

Witness Location & Vetting

Finding the people who matter and making sure you know who they really are before they take the stand. We locate key witnesses, experts, and declarants, then conduct thorough background investigations to understand their credibility, motivations, and potential vulnerabilities. Sometimes the most important witness is the one nobody thought to look for.

Asset Identification & Recovery

Following the money trail, no matter how well it's hidden. We trace assets domestically and internationally, from offshore accounts to shell companies designed to stay invisible. Whether you're enforcing a judgment or heading into settlement talks, knowing what's actually there changes everything.

Evidence Gathering

Going beyond what formal discovery produces. We find the critical documents that weren't turned over, recover digital communications that were thought to be deleted, and piece together circumstantial evidence that tells the real story. The most damaging evidence is usually hiding in plain sight.

Case Assessment

Providing early-stage analysis that helps you understand what you're really dealing with. We conduct objective evaluations of the factual merits before you're too deep into litigation, giving legal teams the intelligence they need to make strategic decisions about how to proceed.

Methodology

Our Process in Practice

Define objectives and locate all relevant sources of information to establish a comprehensive factual baseline.

Meticulously collect and document all evidence and data in a forensically sound manner to ensure its integrity.

Our team synthesizes the collected information, connecting disparate dots, corroborating facts, and extracting the strategic insights that form the core of our intelligence product.

We deliver our findings in a clear, concise, and unambiguous report, tailored to your objectives and designed to provide the actionable clarity needed for decisive action.
